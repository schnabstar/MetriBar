# Privacy Policy

MetriBar does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.** MetriBar contains no third-party SDKs.
- **No accounts and no cloud.** There is no MetriBar server.
- **Network connections go to the Glances servers you add.** The only other connection is the update check, and it is off until you turn it on in Settings › General. It then asks `api.github.com` once a day for the latest MetriBar release, with a plain request that carries no identifier, no cookies and nothing about your servers. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download. MetriBar never downloads or installs anything by itself; it only points you to the release page.
- **Notifications are created on your Mac** from the values your servers report, after you turn them on in Settings › Notifications and allow them in macOS. Nothing is sent to anyone to make them.
- **Your data stays on your Mac.** The server list and preferences are stored in MetriBar's settings on your Mac; passwords are stored in the macOS Keychain. The values MetriBar shows are kept in memory only while the app runs.
- **Diagnostic details** are only created when you click "Copy Details". They are copied to your clipboard and are never sent anywhere by MetriBar. They contain no passwords, user names, process command lines or monitored values, and hide the host name.

To remove all MetriBar data, remove your servers in MetriBar's settings (this also deletes their passwords from the Keychain) and delete the app.
